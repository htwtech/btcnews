# March Was Quiet for Bitcoin Capital, But Tether Was Not

March was quiet for Bitcoin capital. Tether was not.

## The allocations March 2026:

[Utexo](https://x.com/utexocom) — $7.5M seed (Tether, Big Brain Holdings, Portal Ventures, Franklin Templeton, Maven11, Fulgur Ventures, Mar 6)

[Ark Labs](https://x.com/arklabsxyz) — $5.2M seed (Tether, Ego Death Capital, Epoch VC, Sats Ventures, Contribution Capital, Mar 12)

Plan ₿ Lugano — CHF 5M commitment (Tether + City of Lugano, Phase II digital infrastructure, Mar 3)

[Bitwise](https://x.com/BitwiseInvest) — $233K donation from BITB profits to Bitcoin open-source developers (Brink, OpenSats, HRF Bitcoin Development Fund, Mar 4)

[OpenSats](https://x.com/OpenSats) — 5 grants to Bitcoin Core contributors (Mar 11)

## The Real Signal

March was smaller than January by the numbers, but the pattern was clearer. Capital was not moving across Bitcoin broadly. It stayed concentrated around settlement, infrastructure, distribution, and the developer layer underneath it.

To us, that is the real signal. March did not bring a broad return of risk appetite. It showed that even in a quieter month, money still found its way to the parts of the Bitcoin stack that are easiest to justify when the noise drops out.
