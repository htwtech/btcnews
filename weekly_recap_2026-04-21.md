## Infrastructure and Protocol Updates

1️⃣ **Stacks Halving and DeFi Stack Launch**

The Stacks halving has occurred, reducing block rewards to 500 STX per block. This marks a significant reduction in new STX entering circulation. Concurrently, the Stacks DeFi stack has launched, featuring protocols like ZestProtocol for lending, GraniteBTC for institutional loans, HermeticaFi for BTC yield, and bitflow as a DEX, all settling to Bitcoin.

2️⃣ **Starknet Privacy and Protocol Upgrades**

Starknet's Shinobi upgrade is now live, introducing native privacy features. The mainnet will undergo a v0.14.2 upgrade, enabling in-protocol proof verification and further enhancing privacy.

3️⃣ **AmbossTech Multi-Asset Lightning Network**

The Lightning Network has been updated to support multi-asset transactions, expanding its utility and potential use cases across different asset classes.

4️⃣ **BIP-360 and Quantum Resistance**

Bitfinitynet's BIP-360 introduces Pay-to-Merkle-Root (P2MR) to enhance security against quantum attacks, setting a new standard for Bitcoin infrastructure resilience.

5️⃣ **CoreDAO's Quantum Resistance and Yield Infrastructure**

CoreDAO is focusing on building quantum-resistant Bitcoin infrastructure and custom yield solutions for institutions, emphasizing staking architecture and yield engines.

## Ecosystem Growth and Strategic Partnerships

6️⃣ **Goldman Sachs Files for Bitcoin ETF**

Goldman Sachs has filed for a Bitcoin ETF that invests in other Bitcoin ETFs, signaling increased institutional interest and potential market expansion.

7️⃣ **Solv Protocol and UTEXO Integration**

Solv Protocol integrates with UTEXO, enabling programmable Bitcoin yields natively in the Bitcoin Lightning Network. This partnership aims to establish a native liquidity layer directly on Bitcoin’s base layer.

8️⃣ **Plasma and Tether Wallet Integration**

Plasma collaborates with the Stables team to transition users to Plasma One, integrating USDT on Tether Wallet, which is now accessible to over 570 million users.

## Product Launches and Metrics

9️⃣ **Tether Launches Crypto Wallet**

Tether has launched a new crypto wallet, facilitating BTC and stablecoin payments, broadening access to digital currency transactions.

🔟 **MezoNetwork's Liquidity Pools and TVL Milestone**

MezoNetwork has launched liquidity pools and the MUSD Savings Vault, achieving over $170 million in total value locked (TVL), highlighting its growing influence in the BTC DeFi space.

## Security and Protocol Challenges

1️⃣1️⃣ **RiverdotInc Airdrop and Bridge Security Review**

With only one day left for the S4 airdrop, RiverdotInc has paused bridge functions for satUSD and RIVER during an ongoing security review, ensuring user safety and protocol integrity.

1️⃣2️⃣ **MapProtocol Block Production Halt**

MAPO Mainnet is experiencing a block production halt due to a consensus layer inconsistency, with core developers working on a fix. Despite this, the protocol reported significant cross-chain volume and transaction activity.

## Community and Events

1️⃣3️⃣ **Liquid_BTC Simplicity Hackathon**

The Simplicity Hackathon will be held in Turin, Italy, on May 16-17, offering $3,000 for the winner and a Blockstream Jade Plus for top team members, encouraging innovation in Bitcoin development.

1️⃣4️⃣ **Meet the Drapers Filming Event**

In partnership with BitGo, "Meet the Drapers" will film during the Bitcoin Conference, offering participants a chance to pitch for up to $1 million in funding, fostering entrepreneurial growth in the Bitcoin ecosystem.
