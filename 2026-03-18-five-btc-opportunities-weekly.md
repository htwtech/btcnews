# Five BTC Opportunities Worth Looking at This Week

*February 7, 2026*

Here are five BTC opportunities worth looking at this week.

Different setups, different risk profiles. Some focus on fee-based yield, others on staking or infrastructure alignment, but all aim to put BTC to work without fully leaving the Bitcoin ecosystem.

## 1. SatsumaDEX cBTC-WBTC.e Liquidity Pool (APY boosted + points)
Satsuma’s early liquidity pool offer enhanced APRs and points rewards for bootstrapping liquidity. Yield comes from trading fees, temporary APR boosts, and point farming tied to early participation.  
[https://satsuma.exchange/pools](https://satsuma.exchange/pools)

## 2. Lombard Finance LBTC liquid staking (APY 0.4–1%)
Lombard issues LBTC, a liquid, yield-bearing version of BTC backed by Babylon staking. Base yield comes from Babylon emissions converted back into BTC, while LBTC remains deployable across DeFi apps and Lombard’s managed vaults for additional yield.  
[https://lombard.finance/app/stake/](https://lombard.finance/app/stake/)

## 3. Ambosstech Magma Lightning liquidity leasing (APY 4.34%)
A peer-to-peer marketplace for leasing Lightning liquidity. BTC holders earn yield from lease payments and routing fees, with BTC remaining non-custodial and returns tied to real payment activity.  
[https://magma.amboss.tech/buy](https://magma.amboss.tech/buy)

## 4. HermeticaFi hBTC vault (APY 8%)
hBTC is a non-custodial, on-chain BTC yield product that deploys BTC across curated strategies and auto-compounds profits back into BTC. Current yield comes mainly from basis arbitrage and Stacks Dual Stacking.  
[https://hermetica.fi](https://hermetica.fi)

## 5. xWBTC/WBTC Troves liquidity strategy on Starknet (APY 2.24%)
An automated LP strategy that deploys xWBTC/WBTC into an Ekubo concentrated liquidity pool. Positions are continuously rebalanced around the market price, with all trading fees and incentives auto-compounded.  
[https://app.troves.fi/strategy/ekubo_cl_xwbtcwbtc?tab=manage](https://app.troves.fi/strategy/ekubo_cl_xwbtcwbtc?tab=manage)
