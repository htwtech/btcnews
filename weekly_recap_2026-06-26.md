## Infrastructure and Protocol Updates

1️⃣ **BOB Gateway Launches with Competitive Fees**

@build_on_bob has launched the BOB Gateway, offering zero protocol fees and averaging a total fee of ~0.025%, the lowest among non-custodial protocols. This positions BOB Gateway as a cost-effective solution for BTC transactions.

2️⃣ **ERC-7730 Introduction by @bitfinitynet**

@bitfinitynet introduces ERC-7730, a new standard for clear signing. This protocol change translates transaction data into plain language before approval, enhancing transparency and user experience.

3️⃣ **New Liquidity Model by @Endur**

@Endur has introduced a new liquidity model for staking, allowing faster withdrawals independent of the native unbonding period. This change aims to enhance liquidity and user flexibility in staking operations.

4️⃣ **Decentralized Sequencing by @GOATNetwork**

@GOATNetwork has implemented decentralized sequencing, ensuring that transaction ordering and liveness are not dependent on a single operator. This design supports economic infrastructure for agents in the open economy.

5️⃣ **SEV Subnets on @dfinity's Internet Computer**

@dfinity has launched SEV Subnets, a feature designed to enhance data security on the Internet Computer. Users can now register an email for recovery access, improving security measures for data management.

## Product Launches and Integrations

1️⃣ **Alpha Selini Vault by @Bedrock_DeFi**

@Bedrock_DeFi has launched the Alpha Selini Vault, providing institutional credit yield on BTC directly to retail users without speculation. This marks the launch of Bedrock 2.0, opening new avenues for BTC yield.

2️⃣ **Bitcoin Staking Launch on @Stacks**

@Stacks is set to launch Bitcoin Staking in Q3, in collaboration with @UTXOmgmt. This initiative marks a significant milestone with $3B in total transaction volume on Stacks.

3️⃣ **satUSD Integration by @RiverdotInc**

@RiverdotInc has integrated satUSD with Melon Cash, enabling seamless transfers and payments directly from the satUSD balance. This integration facilitates daily spending with enhanced ease and accessibility.

4️⃣ **Launch of Taproot Assets v0.8 by @lightning**

@lightning has released Taproot Assets v0.8 along with a new SDK for developers. This launch aims to onboard stablecoins onto Bitcoin, expanding the ecosystem's capabilities.

5️⃣ **Verifiable Bitcoin Accounts by @TheTNetwork**

@TheTNetwork has launched Verifiable Bitcoin Accounts (VBA), allowing institutions to manage Bitcoin with their existing Qualified Custodian. This product launch supports institutional adoption of Bitcoin.

## Ecosystem Growth and Market Updates

1️⃣ **Rootstock Dominance and Explorer Updates**

@rootstock_io has reported a 4x growth in dominance across Bitcoin sidechains since 2025. Additionally, updates to the Rootstock Explorer enhance transaction readability and contract workflow efficiency.

2️⃣ **Launch of Bitcoin-backed Borrowing by @Babylon Labs**

@Babylon Labs has introduced Bitcoin-backed borrowing, where native BTC is locked in a Taproot script. This allows users to borrow assets like USDC or USDT, achieving over $200M in deposits within three months.

3️⃣ **Integration with Telegram by @TON**

@TON has integrated its decentralized blockchain with Telegram, designed for a billion users. This integration represents a significant step in blockchain accessibility and user engagement.

4️⃣ **Launch of DA8 Fund by @Draper VC**

@Draper VC has launched the DA8 fund with $300M for early-stage investments. The fund focuses on technically serious founders disrupting industries that have remained unchanged for over 20 years.

5️⃣ **BlackRock's BITA Launch by @Lombard_Finance**

@Lombard_Finance reports BlackRock's launch of BITA, the first US covered-call Bitcoin ETF targeting 15–25% annual yield. This development signals growing institutional interest in Bitcoin-based financial products.

## Security and Performance Updates

1️⃣ **Highest Risk Rating by @EtherFi**

@EtherFi has achieved the highest risk rating from @CredoraNetwork, highlighting their commitment to user fund safety and security in the DeFi space.

2️⃣ **Private USDC Features by @Starknet**

@Starknet has launched private USDC features, allowing users to shield, unshield, and send USDC privately without exposing balances. This product launch enhances privacy and confidentiality in DeFi transactions.
