BOB (Build on Bitcoin) is a Hybrid Layer-2 solution that merges Bitcoin’s security with Ethereum’s flexibility, enabling developers to build applications like Bitcoin DeFi, Bitcoin staking, Cross-chain BTC Yield products, NFTs, and more. Powered by BitVM, BOB will allow BTC to enter DeFi in a trust-minimized way. It also operates BTC-secured bridges to Ethereum and other chains, offering BTC holders secure, decentralized access to Bitcoin DeFi. With low fees and fast transactions, BOB aims to be a gateway for developers and users entering the Bitcoin DeFi ecosystem.

Since its mainnet launch in May 2024, BOB has processed 40M+ transactions from over 550,000 active users, with more than 1.1 million unique wallet addresses having interacted with the chain, with more than 100 partner projects building on the network.

## Investors and funding

BOB has received investments from notable backers including Castle Island Ventures, Coinbase Ventures, Mechanism Capital, and Bankless Ventures. It has also seen support from individual investors like Stani from Aave, Dan Held, DeFi Dad and Andrew Kang, along with many other prominent figures.

## Project background

BOB is an open-source project started in 2023 by [Distributed Crafts](https://www.distributedcrafts.xyz), a UK research & development company founded in 2019, with BOB Co-founders Alexei and Dominik holding a PHD in computer science from Imperial College London.

BOB aims to facilitate experimentation, provide real-world impact, and enhance freedom of choice for developers and users without relying on Bitcoin soft forks. By merging Bitcoin’s security with Ethereum’s smart contract capabilities, BOB envisions a future where decentralized applications on Bitcoin are both powerful and accessible.

## Problem the project solves

Bitcoin was created as a decentralized, transparent, and censorship-resistant payment system. Over time, smart contract platforms like Ethereum enabled DeFi, NFTs, DAOs, and other innovations, while Bitcoin has lagged in developer activity and programmability.

Despite this, Bitcoin remains dominant with a $1 trillion market cap, 300 million users, and leading trading volume. Yet, its DeFi presence is minimal — just 0.1% TVL relative to market cap, compared to Ethereum’s 30%, highlighting a 300x gap.

Efforts to bring native smart contracts to Bitcoin via forks or protocol upgrades have failed, as the Bitcoin developer community resists major functional changes. As a result, BOB emerges as the most viable path to unlock BTC’s potential in DeFi.

## Technology Stack

- Hybrid of Bitcoin’s Proof-of-Work for security and Ethereum’s OP Stack for execution.
- Trust-minimized Bitcoin bridge via BitVM2
- Bitcoin security via BitVM2 + Bitcoin Secured Network via Babylon
- Supports Solidity, making it easy for developers to migrate existing Ethereum apps to BOB.

## Strengths and weaknesses

### Strengths:

- Doesn’t rely on Op-codes like OP CAT, OP CTV etc, pragmatic approach for adoption.
- Trust minimized BTC bridges to BTC and Ethereum.
- Easy adoption for developers due to EVM compatibility.
- Supports multiple use cases, Bitcoin DeFi natives, Cross-chain Bitcoin yield products, Bitcoin Staking, NFTs etc.
- Leader in BitVM innovation, Co-author of BitVM2 paper
- Organically high Bitcoin TVL + DeFi userbase
- First hybrid ZK rollup between Bitcoin and Ethereum — technical moat established
- Won Bitcoin Research Award 2025 for BitVM2 paper


### Weaknesses:

- ZK Rollups are not possible for practical implementation on Bitcoin today, this is the same conundrum for Citrea, Alpen etc.

- BOB represents a significant step forward in making blockchain development more secure and efficient by uniting the best features of Bitcoin and Ethereum. It offers a robust platform for decentralized applications while ensuring low fees and strong security.
