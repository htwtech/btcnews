## Infrastructure and Protocol Updates

1️⃣ **BOB Gateway Expands BTC Wrappers and Stablecoin Routes**

@build_on_bob has integrated more BTC wrappers and stablecoin routes, including SolvBTC, LBTC, RLUSD, and DAI. This update enhances refund efficiency for incomplete orders and strengthens security measures.

2️⃣ **Rootstock Enhances Security with Parallel Transaction Execution**

@RootstockLabs is testing Parallel Transaction Execution on their testnet, leveraging Bitcoin’s hashrate through merged mining. This is slated for inclusion in Cardamom 10.0.0, extending Bitcoin’s security to Rootstock.

3️⃣ **Core Mainnet Hardfork v1.0.26 Launched**

@CoreEcosystem has successfully launched a hardfork on their mainnet, addressing reward issuance issues, closing vulnerabilities, and burning over 150M CORE from the supply.

4️⃣ **Alpend Protocol Upgrade**

@alpend integrates multiparty hosting from Canton Loop and a Decentralization Manager from BitSafe Finance, all secured by a Halborn Security-audited codebase.

5️⃣ **GOATNetwork Advances Post-Quantum Migration**

@GOATNetwork continues its post-quantum migration with Ziren, the proof system under the GOAT BitVM3 bridge. They also published an explainer on SHRINCS, a draft signature scheme for Bitcoin’s post-quantum migration.

## Product Launches and Partnerships

1️⃣ **Avalon Labs Launches On-Chain Capital Markets**

@Avalon_Labs is making Bitcoin holdings productive with the launch of on-chain capital markets, focusing on value movement and settlement infrastructure.

2️⃣ **Zest Protocol’s Levered Bitcoin Staking Vault Goes Live**

@ZestProtocol has launched a Levered Bitcoin Staking Vault, increasing its capacity to 20 BTC due to high demand, automating Bitcoin staking yield processes.

3️⃣ **Stacks Bitcoin Staking Launch and Partnership Extension**

@Stacks has launched Bitcoin Staking with the Genesis Bond and extended its partnership with @HashKeyCloud as a Bitcoin Staking launch partner. STX is now live on Bullish, expanding institutional access.

4️⃣ **Hemi Introduces New Liquidity Farming Opportunities**

@HemiFinance offers new farming opportunities across HEMI, VUSD, hemiBTC, WBTC, ETH, and USDC.e, with incentives in USDC.

5️⃣ **FunctionBTC Partners with Bybit**

@FunctionBTC collaborates with @Bybit_Official to provide BTC holders with secure and competitive yields.

## Ecosystem Growth and Metrics

1️⃣ **Stacks Ecosystem Overview**

@tenero_io reports significant growth in the Stacks ecosystem with a DEX volume of $20.41M (+369% MoM), 1,284 active traders (+8% MoM), and 174,380 trades (+51% MoM).

2️⃣ **Surge’s Taproot Vault Metrics**

@SurgeProtocol reports that 21.14 BTC have moved through their non-custodial Taproot vaults, with 14.94 BTC locked, earning a 4.7% average supply APY for USDC Yield.

3️⃣ **EtherFi's August Performance Metrics**

@ether_fi reports staking over 158,662 ETH, a $5.23B TVL, and significant product launches, including tokenized stocks and metals trading, and a new @aave market on @Optimism.

4️⃣ **MapProtocol August Monthly Report**

@MapProtocol highlights upgrades to their Radar service, improvements in cross-chain crediting flow, and a $100.9M cross-chain volume over the last 30 days.

5️⃣ **EtherFi Cash Transaction Milestone**

@ether_fi has achieved 10,000,000 transactions with EtherFi Cash, marking a significant milestone in their operational metrics.
