## Infrastructure and Integrations

1️⃣ **BOB Gateway Expands Reach**
   - @build_on_bob Gateway integrated with @AcrossProtocol, enabling native BTC swaps to settle across more EVM chains with speed and cost efficiency.
   - HyperEVM support added, providing a seamless onramp for native BTC trading on Hyperliquid.

2️⃣ **RiverdotInc and Melon Cash Collaboration**
   - Melon Cash now supports payments with satUSD via a Visa virtual card, extending availability to over 10 countries.

3️⃣ **Hemi's Strategic Moves**
   - Hemi is live on Lunar Finance, allowing users to tunnel assets from EVM or SVM and access BTC-native yield without wrapping.
   - Integration with Endless Domains enhances decentralized identity accessibility on Hemi.

4️⃣ **AlpenLabs Testnet III Progress**
   - Partner integrations are underway with over 21 launch partners preparing for mainnet integration.

## Protocol and Product Launches

5️⃣ **Stacks PoX-5 Activation**
   - The PoX-5 hardfork is live, marking a significant upgrade to Proof of Transfer, enabling Bitcoin Staking.
   - Stakers must restake before block 962,050 to continue earning BTC yield.

6️⃣ **Starknet's New Offerings**
   - Launch of DeFi’s first private liquid staking for Bitcoin, powered by @EndurFi, offering shielded strkBTC staking.
   - OpusMoney's Auto-Topup feature now live, allowing users to automate position management.

7️⃣ **Lombard Finance's New Strategy**
   - Launched a Bitcoin Onchain Credit Strategy with Flow Traders as a pilot partner, enabling stablecoin borrowing against BTC collateral.

8️⃣ **Zest Protocol's Innovations**
   - Introduced BTC yield strategies with over 800 BTC earning yield and launched Stacks Vaults, targeting a 6-8% yield on Bitcoin Staking.

9️⃣ **Bedrock DeFi Update**
   - Vault Redemption Delay adjusted from 7 days to 14 days, effective September 6, 2026.

## Partnerships and Strategic Alignments

🔟 **Visa Joins Canton Network**
   - Visa joins @CantonFdn as a General Member, indicating a long-term commitment to the network.

11️⃣ **Stacks and Ryder Wallet Partnership**
   - Stacks collaborates with Ryder Wallet for staking STX, enhancing the ecosystem's staking capabilities.

12️⃣ **AlpenLabs and Stakely Partnership**
   - @Stakely_io will secure BTC deposits and withdrawals, supporting financial activities on Alpen.

## Market Metrics and Insights

13️⃣ **Bitcoin Market Dominance Surge**
   - Bitcoin's market dominance increased from 40% to 61%, with expectations to reach 70% post-next halving cycle.

14️⃣ **Avalon Labs' Institutional Inflows**

15️⃣ **TheTNetwork's Growth**
