## Infrastructure and Protocol Developments

1️⃣ **BOB Gateway Expands Swap Options**

@build_on_bob has rolled out new swap capabilities, enabling $BTC ↔ $RLUSD transactions on Ethereum. RLUSD is @Ripple's NYDFS-licensed stablecoin. Additionally, $BTC ↔ $USD1 swaps are now available on both @Ethereum and @BNBCHAIN.

2️⃣ **Lombard Finance Shifts Yield Strategy**

@LombardFinance has transitioned $LBTC's yield source to an institutional covered-call options strategy, managed by Bitwise. This strategic shift aims to deliver a 2.5% net APY, moving away from emissions to options-based yield generation.

3️⃣ **Rootstock Introduces rBTC**

@RootstockLabs has launched rBTC, a representation of Bitcoin on the Rootstock network. This allows Bitcoin to be utilized for lending, borrowing, payments, liquidity provision, and smart contract execution.

4️⃣ **MapProtocol Mainnet Goes Live**

@MapProtocol's mainnet is now operational, facilitating open cross-chain swaps and enhancing interoperability across blockchain networks.

5️⃣ **Starknet Bridges Security Update**

@Starknet has temporarily disabled bridging due to a security issue. They are working on a new integration to restore service and introduce CREDI to facilitate private credit markets on the network.

## Ecosystem Growth and Partnerships

1️⃣ **FunctionBTC Partners with Bybit**

@functionbtc has teamed up with Bybit On-Chain Earn to offer competitive BTC yields, leveraging $FBTC infrastructure to enhance user returns.

2️⃣ **Riverdot Enables satUSD Payments in Brazil**

@Riverdot has integrated satUSD for payments, allowing users to pay at any PIX QR in Brazil and settle in BRL through Yodlpay.

3️⃣ **Citrea Partners with MoonPay**

@citrea_xyz has integrated ctUSD with MoonPay, enabling users to participate in the Bitcoin economy with direct on-ramps available in the US and over 120 countries.

4️⃣ **Babylon Labs and Emirates Partnership**

@BabylonLabs has announced that Emirates, the world’s most profitable airline, is now accepting digital assets for flight bookings, marking a significant milestone in mainstream adoption.

## Product Launches and Updates

1️⃣ **Zest Protocol Launches Bitcoin Collateral Vaults**

@ZestProtocol has introduced Bitcoin Collateral Vaults, allowing users to borrow USDCx against sBTC and earn rewards in BTC. This development brings native BTC collateralization to Bitcoin L1.

2️⃣ **Hemi Launches VUSD Stablecoin**

@HemiFinance has launched VUSD, a native stablecoin designed for a 24/7 onchain economy. The frxUSD/VUSD pool is now live on Curve, supporting liquidity deposits and swaps.

3️⃣ **BounceBit Launches Borobudur**

@BounceBit has unveiled Borobudur, offering access to 0% interest $BB credit. An upgrade for the BounceBit Chain is scheduled for August 23, 09:00 UTC.

4️⃣ **TheTNetwork Introduces Verifiable Bitcoin Accounts**

@TheTNetwork has launched Verifiable Bitcoin Accounts (VBA), enabling Bitcoin-backed lending desks to source working capital while maintaining auditable collateral on the Bitcoin Network.

## Metrics and Achievements

1️⃣ **Stacks Report on Bloomberg Terminals**

@Stacks' TTF report is now available on Bloomberg Terminals as part of Blockworks' industry-standard disclosure framework. The ecosystem has reached a $6 billion trading volume milestone.

2️⃣ **Midl Reports Strong BTC Inflows**

@midl_xyz noted that US spot ETFs absorbed over 14,000 BTC in five days, marking the strongest weekly inflows since mid-April.
