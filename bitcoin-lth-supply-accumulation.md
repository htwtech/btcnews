Long-term holders are back to doing what they do best: absorbing supply and sitting on their hands. 

As of April 14, the 30-day LTH Supply Change is sitting at a massive +356.6K BTC, pushing the total long-term holder supply back up to the 15.06M BTC mark. 

## The 155-Day Cohort is Swelling

The real signal here isn't just that the monthly change flipped positive—it's the sheer size of the move. Over 350,000 coins have officially crossed the 155-day aging threshold over the last four weeks alone. 

That tells us the heavy distribution phase the market was grinding through in late 2025 is fading out. Coins are spending way less time bouncing around in active circulation and are instead making their way into deep storage setups controlled by entities that notoriously refuse to panic sell. 

## A Shrinking Liquid Float

For anyone watching the on-chain dynamics, that is the primary takeaway. It's not about whatever immediate bounce we see on lower timeframes. It's about structural market conditions.

Less liquid supply is actively making its way back to the market right now. Any new bids stepping up have to fight over a constantly shrinking float, and the entities holding the bulk of the inventory are clearly in no rush to offload it.
