# Capital Flows: Treasuries and ETFs Dominate Late April

Private rounds were light again, but the broader flow stayed active. This week, capital showed up mostly through treasury funding, public-market wrappers, and regulated BTC products.

## The Corporate Bid Stays Aggressive

The flow between April 24 and April 30 highlights a market driven by balance sheets rather than venture capital. Metaplanet tapped the debt markets with a $50M zero-interest bond raise via EVO FUND, specifically structured to finance additional BTC purchases. MicroStrategy kept up the pressure, acquiring another 3,273 BTC for roughly $255M at an average execution price of $77,906. Meanwhile, Strive added roughly 789 BTC to their stack, bringing their total holdings up to 14,557 BTC.

## Public Wrappers Soak Up the Volume

The institutional flow into listed products matched the corporate side. Broad Bitcoin investment products registered $1.2B in weekly inflows, with Bitcoin standalone vehicles accounting for $933M of that figure. U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs posted $823.7M in weekly net inflows, marking their fourth consecutive positive week. To put that in perspective, the April 14–24 streak alone ripped for $2.12B.

This was not a week of headline private VC deals, but it was still a massive week for Bitcoin capital. The flow is moving strictly through bonds, treasury programs, ETFs, and listed products rather than early-stage protocol rounds. 

More Bitcoin demand is arriving through formal market structures first. The challenge for the Bitcoin-native side remains proving it can eventually turn that massive wave of passive exposure into active lending, settlement, collateral, payments, and yield.
