# BTC Is Recovering Through Flows First

BTC has recovered enough for the market to start talking about momentum again.

But the cleaner read this week is that the recovery is still being led by flows before it is being confirmed on-chain.

## Spot Bitcoin ETF On-Chain Flows in BTC

![Spot Bitcoin ETF On-Chain Flows in BTC](https://fiphsgznopoesjaxwkwz.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/networks/screen-1-2204.png)

ETF demand is back, but the flow is still uneven.

ETF demand has clearly improved. Spot Bitcoin ETF on-chain flows have turned more active again, and that matters. This is the part of the market that can stabilize price quickly when risk appetite comes back.

But it is not the whole story.

## Percentage of BTC Supply in Profit

![Percentage of BTC Supply in Profit](https://fiphsgznopoesjaxwkwz.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/networks/screem-3-2204.png)

Profitability has recovered from the lows, but the market is far from euphoric.

Supply in profit has also recovered from the March lows, which tells us the market is no longer in the same stress regime. More holders are back above cost basis. Forced pessimism has eased.

But this is recovery from stress, not excess.

## Number of Active Addresses on the Bitcoin Network

![Number of Active Addresses on the Bitcoin Network](https://fiphsgznopoesjaxwkwz.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/networks/screem-4-2204.png)

Network participation is still sitting near the lower end of the yearly range.

Active addresses remain near the lower end of the past year’s range, even after the recent bounce. That is the part we think matters. Price can recover through ETF demand and derivatives positioning before base-layer activity fully confirms it, but that kind of recovery is usually more fragile.

## Transactions on the Bitcoin Network

![Transactions on the Bitcoin Network](https://fiphsgznopoesjaxwkwz.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/networks/screem-5-2204.png)

Transactions are recovering, but activity has not yet translated into stronger economic throughput.

Transactions are the counterpoint. They have recovered strongly since February and are now near the upper end of the recent range. So the network is not inactive. But higher transaction count alone does not prove a broad demand reset, especially while monthly on-chain volume is still well below the Q4 2025 highs.

So the setup is not as clean as the price action makes it look.

The flow side of the market looks better. The profitability side looks better. But the on-chain activity picture is still mixed: more transactions, weaker active address breadth, and softer economic throughput.

In our view, that makes this recovery real, but not fully confirmed.

BTC is stronger than it was a few weeks ago. The bid has returned. But until on-chain participation and settlement volume improve alongside ETF flows, the move still looks more flow-led than structurally broad.
