# Bitcoin Recovered, but MVRV Still Isn’t Overheated

Bitcoin has recovered from the April lows, but MVRV Z-Score is still sitting around 0.85.

That is the interesting part.

The market no longer looks like pure stress, but it also does not look overheated. This is not the kind of chart you usually see when BTC is deep into a euphoric premium phase.

For us, this looks like a market where confidence is returning faster than excess.

## Price recovered faster than valuation stretched

BTC price has already moved off the lows, but the valuation signal has not expanded aggressively yet.

That matters because MVRV Z-Score is useful for reading whether Bitcoin is trading at an extreme premium compared with its realized value. When the metric runs too hot, the market usually becomes more crowded, more reflexive, and more vulnerable to leverage-driven corrections.

Right now, the setup looks cleaner.

Price has moved, but valuation has not stretched too far. Bitcoin is no longer in the same stress zone as April, but it is also not showing the kind of overheated structure that usually appears near euphoric phases.

## The market is recovering, not overheating

This is the difference between a healthy recovery and an aggressive speculative expansion.

In a speculative phase, price and valuation usually accelerate together. Momentum pulls in new buyers, MVRV expands quickly, and the market starts pricing BTC with a heavier premium.

That is not what this chart is showing yet.

Instead, Bitcoin looks like it is rebuilding confidence without fully moving into excess. The recovery is visible, but the valuation backdrop still leaves room before the market starts looking crowded.

## The next signal to watch

The next important signal is whether MVRV starts expanding together with price.

If BTC continues higher and MVRV rises sharply, the market would be moving into a more speculative phase. That would not automatically be bearish, but it would mean the risk profile is changing.

If price continues to hold or grind higher while MVRV stays controlled, the recovery would look more sustainable. That would suggest Bitcoin is being repriced without the same level of aggressive premium expansion.

BitBoard’s view: BTC is in a cleaner zone right now. Recovery is confirmed, but the market does not look overheated yet. The key question is whether the next leg is backed by steady demand or turns into a more crowded speculative move.
