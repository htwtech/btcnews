# Ecosystem Map: Stacks

Over the last cycle, Stacks added more recognizable access rails around BTC and sBTC.

By late 2025 / early 2026, the ecosystem had USDCx from Circle on mainnet, with Bitflow, Granite, Leather, Xverse, Asigna, Fordefi, and Zest integrated from day one. It also expanded wallet connectivity through WalletConnect, institutional access through BitGo, Copper, and later Fireblocks, and liquidity venues through the sBTC listing on MEXC.

That gives the map a different shape.

Around sBTC and USDCx, Stacks now has wallets, custody, stablecoin infrastructure, market access, and application liquidity lining up in the same stack.

That is why the ecosystem feels more connected right now. The map reads like a financial layer where BTC capital can move through multiple products and access points inside the same environment.
