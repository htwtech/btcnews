# BTC Ecosystem Weekly Digest

*April 8, 2026*

While retail is distracted by short-term price action, the infrastructure layer is aggressively pushing new primitives to mainnet. From zero-slippage swaps to bridging TradFi liquidity, here is what actually matters this week:

## 1. BOB and Instant Swaps
They launched a product offering instant, fixed-rate BTC swaps. We are talking execution under 10 seconds with zero slippage. On top of that, they integrated 133 PRs in March alone, so the dev momentum is printing.

## 2. Rootstock - Vetiver 9.0.0 Upgrade
Consensus changes for Vetiver 9.0.0 are officially approved. Mainnet activation is locked for block 8,804,200 (around May 4th), tightening protocol security and execution efficiency.

## 3. Starknet - Expanding the DeFi Stack
Starkzap v2 is live, adding native modules for bridging, swaps, DCA, and confidential transfers. They are also future-proofing the network with a post-quantum proving system and recursive circuits.

## 4. OP_NET - Smart Contracts on L1
Native L1 contracts are here. With over $26M already flowing through the system, Motoswap deployed a full DeFi stack natively on Bitcoin's base layer, meaning you can swap, stake, and farm without sketchy wrappers.

## 5. Babylon and Trustless Vaults
Trustless Bitcoin Vaults are live, unlocking native borrowing power on Aave v4. You build a vault, lock the BTC, and bridge raw collateral directly into Ethereum's DeFi ecosystem without centralized custodians.

## 6. Core - Yield on the London Stock Exchange
Yield-bearing Bitcoin is officially trading on the LSE, powered by Core. Traditional investors can now ape into BTC yields using legacy brokerage accounts, completely bypassing crypto wallets.

## 7. Solv Protocol - Institutional Rails
They partnered with Gumi Cryptos and The Spartan Group to build out the compliance and risk-management rails. They are specifically targeting Institutional DeFi to safely park heavy capital in Bitcoin Finance.

## 8. Utexo - Privacy-First Payments
They are tackling the privacy-versus-compliance dilemma on Lightning. You pass a privacy-first KYC check using portable ZK-proofs to keep the regulators happy, but the actual transaction data remains completely shielded.

---

Retail is focusing on the noise, while the protocols are building serious gateways for real money and native L1 utility.
