# Botanix: What Bitcoin Finance Actually Means

Botanix dropped positioning around "Bitcoin Finance," and honestly, it's less about features than drawing a line around what doing finance with Bitcoin means.

Trust assumptions shift when you wrap BTC, because custodians (and often bridge/issuance rails) sit between you and actual Bitcoin. Engineering culture shifts. The whole relationship between you and your collateral changes.

We see DeFi platforms anchor Bitcoin as settlement, feed it through bridges and custodial wrappers. When BTC gets wrapped, you're holding an IOU that might track Bitcoin's value, but the security model is different now. The trust model is different. You're not holding Bitcoin anymore, you're holding a derivative of Bitcoin.

Bitcoin's non-Turing-complete scripting shaped an engineering culture that prioritizes simplicity, auditability, minimizing trust. Colored Coins in 2012, atomic swaps, state-channel research - all because Bitcoin developers couldn't just iterate like Ethereum. That forced progress to move differently. Slower, yes. But Botanix argues progress is more intentional, more security-first. And that's what they're actually building around.

Layers attached to Bitcoin plus trust minimization plus native BTC as collateral, no shortcut tokens that hollow out security - that's a different engineering culture than "Ethereum mechanics anchored to Bitcoin," because Bitcoin and Ethereum made opposite bets about what trade-offs matter most.

Constrain them? Yes. And we're watching which teams actually lean into that discipline versus chasing yield campaigns.
