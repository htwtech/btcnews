# Bitcoin Dominance Shows Risk Is Still Off

Risk is off. Bitcoin still holds the center.

BTC dominance is sitting near 58.4%, while ETH dominance is around 9.3% and market sentiment remains deep in fear. That combination says more than the daily candle.

The market is weak, but capital is not rotating down the risk curve. Alts are not taking leadership. Bitcoin is still where the market keeps its core exposure.

## Dominance matters more during fear

BTC dominance is most useful when the market is under stress.

In strong risk-on conditions, capital usually moves further out: ETH, majors, mid-caps, small caps, narratives, and higher-beta trades. That is when dominance can fall because investors are willing to take more risk.

This is not that environment.

Dominance holding near 58% during fear suggests the market is not looking for more risk. It is staying concentrated in the asset the market trusts first when risk comes off.

That does not mean Bitcoin is immune to downside.

It means Bitcoin is still the center of the market structure.

## This is not an alt-led market

The current setup does not look like an alt-led phase.

For that to change, BTC dominance would need to break lower with strength while capital rotates into ETH and broader alt sectors in a sustained way.

So far, the signal is different.

ETH dominance is still low around 9.3%, sentiment remains defensive, and Bitcoin continues to hold the largest share of market attention.

That points to a market where traders may still be active, but capital is not aggressively moving into higher-risk assets.

## BitBoard take

Until BTC dominance breaks lower with strength, this is not an alt-led market.

It is still Bitcoin at the center.

The market can be weak and still Bitcoin-dominant at the same time. That is what makes the current structure important: risk is off, but the core exposure remains BTC.

For now, dominance is saying one thing clearly.

The market is not ready to move down the risk curve yet.
