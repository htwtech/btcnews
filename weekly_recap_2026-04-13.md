## Infrastructure and Protocol Updates

1️⃣ **BOB Gateway Enhancements**

BOB Gateway has increased its transaction limit to 2 BTC, with plans for further increases. The launch of fixed-rate and instant BTC swaps promises to enhance user experience, with fee-free swaps available for frequent users at launch.

2️⃣ **Rootstock's Latest Developments**

Rootstock released the Vetiver 9.0.1 patch, resolving a debug_traceTransaction issue. Node operators using tracing/debugging are advised to upgrade. The Aori integration on Rootstock now provides users access to DeFi opportunities backed by Bitcoin, with over $600M settled in volume.

3️⃣ **Starknet Upgrades**

The Starknet v0.14.2 upgrade vote is underway, introducing SNIP-36, SNIP-37, and SNIP-13, and preparing the network for decentralized validation. This release focuses on enabling in-protocol S-Two proof verification and revising storage economics.

4️⃣ **Lightning Network Enhancements**

Lightning has released the LND Helm chart for managing nodes in production and is hiring an AI Product Engineer to develop machine-scale payment products.

Core has integrated Bitcoin yield strategies, launched Bitcoin neobank features, and partnered with banks and custodians. This enables one-click, permissionless yield strategies and real-time settlement, enhancing Bitcoin's financial utility.
