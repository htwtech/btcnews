# Rootstock: secure smart contracts on Bitcoin

Rootstock is a Bitcoin sidechain that aims to bring smart contract capabilities to Bitcoin, combining Bitcoin's security with Ethereum-compatible smart contracts. It uses merged mining, meaning miners can mine Rootstock while mining Bitcoin, and offers a seamless way to interact with DeFi applications using Bitcoin-backed assets.

Rootstock originated in 2015, with its mainnet launching in January 2018. The platform aims to create a permissionless, secure sidechain to broaden Bitcoin's use cases beyond simple transactions. By leveraging Bitcoin's Proof-of-Work security and supporting Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM) compatibility, Rootstock provides developers with familiar tools for building on the platform.

## The Founders of Rootstock

Rootstock was co-founded by Sergio Lerner, Diego Gutierrez Zaldivar, Adrian Eidelman, Gabriel Kurman, and Ruben Altman, each bringing unique expertise and vision to the project.

- Sergio Lerner is known for his significant contributions to the Bitcoin protocol and his security research related to Ethereum protocol and clients. As a cybersecurity expert, he discovered and disclosed 9 vulnerabilities in Bitcoin and 5 vulnerabilities in Ethereum protocol and clients. His vision for Rootstock was to integrate Bitcoin's security with the versatility of Ethereum's smart contracts, addressing real-world economic challenges.

- Diego Gutierrez Zaldivar has been a key figure in promoting Bitcoin's transformative potential. He is co-founder of LABITCONF, the longest-running Bitcoin conference in the world, and one of the early Bitcoin community builders in Latin America. His innovative approach helped turn the concepts of blockchain into Rootstock, a platform designed to foster financial inclusion.

Together, these founders combined their expertise to create a platform that not only solves technical problems but also addresses socio-economic challenges, particularly in regions like Latin America and Africa.

Rootstock's journey is a testament to collaboration and shared vision. Under the leadership of Sergio Lerner, Diego Gutierrez Zaldivar, and the broader founding team, Rootstock was crafted to tackle both technical blockchain aspects and socio-economic challenges. The platform's unique approach to merged mining, smart contracts, and its two-way peg system reflects the founders' commitment to building a secure, efficient, and user-friendly blockchain solution.

## PowPeg functionaries and ecosystem participants

BlockVenture, Luxor, pNetwork, Collider, RootstockLabs, and Xapo serve as PowPeg functionaries and contribute to the platform's development. Sovryn is a major protocol built on the Rootstock ecosystem.

## Establishing a new ecosystem — Rootstock's journey since launch

Since its mainnet launch in January 2018, Rootstock reports 100% uptime with no chain reset and no reported network outage. The PowPeg has had no security breach or loss of funds since early 2018. In this time, it has nurtured a vibrant ecosystem of decentralized applications (dApps), revolutionizing how people interact with financial services. Platforms like Money on Chain, Sovryn, Tropykus, and Babelfish have emerged, offering an array of services from minting Bitcoin-backed stablecoins to trading, lending, borrowing, and bridging into other ecosystems.

Rootstock's ecosystem stands out for its commitment to decentralization. Many core Rootstock DeFi apps are non-custodial and permissionless, and several do not require KYC, making them accessible to anyone with an internet connection. This open and inclusive financial ecosystem is not just a technological achievement but a realization of Rootstock's founding vision of fostering financial inclusion and empowerment on a global scale.

## Key features

- Secured by over 80% of Bitcoin's hashrate through merged mining.
- EVM-compatible for easy deployment of Ethereum smart contracts.
- Two-way Bitcoin peg (PowPeg) allows users to convert BTC to rBTC and back.
- Supports DeFi applications using Bitcoin-backed rBTC for swaps, gas fees, and more.
- Rootstock supports interoperability with Bitcoin and multiple EVM and non-EVM ecosystems via bridge infrastructure, including access from 20+ chains.
- PowPeg uses tamper-proof hardware security modules (HSMs) for added security.

## Technology stack

- **Security model:** Bitcoin PoW inherited via merge-mining.
- **Blockchain Architecture:** Bitcoin sidechain, often described as a Bitcoin Layer 2.
- **Smart Contract Language:** Supports Solidity, allowing Ethereum developers to easily deploy contracts on Rootstock.
- **Notable Technologies:** PowPeg for secure pegging, rollup-based scaling tooling available through ecosystem partners, HSM attestation for added security, and ongoing PowHSM / hardware-attestation work, including SGX-based components in the roadmap.

## Project status and roadmap

Rootstock is live as a Bitcoin sidechain with ongoing development in improving cross-chain functionality and scalability.

### Roadmap highlights as of March 2026:

- Lovell network upgrade activated March 2025, delivering consensus improvements, developer tooling enhancements, and a ~60% gas fee reduction.
- Reed network upgrade went live in 2025, introducing Segwit PowPeg support and approximately 60% reduction in peg-out costs.
- LayerZero/Stargate integration enabled bridging of USDC, USDT, and WETH from 20+ chains.
- Boltz integration live on mainnet, enabling Lightning-based swaps into Rootstock.
- Union Bridge / BitVMX in active development as the next-generation trust-minimized bridge path.
