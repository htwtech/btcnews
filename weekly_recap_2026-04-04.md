## Market Overview

The Bitcoin ecosystem continues to evolve with significant product launches, protocol changes, and strategic integrations. This week, we delve into the latest updates that are shaping the future of Bitcoin and its surrounding technologies.

### Product Launches and Integrations

1️⃣ **BOB Enhancements**  
BOB has merged 133 PRs in March, including 27 new features. Key updates include improved pricing and routing in the swap app, faster order status, and support for ETH, USDC, and USDT on BSC. Notably, they have developed a design for trustless Bitcoin inscriptions, allowing projects to inscribe art, collections, or data on Bitcoin without holding BTC or managing private keys.

2️⃣ **River's Expansion to Base**  
River is now live on Base, providing direct access to Ethereum's leading L2 ecosystem. This integration includes $RIVER spot trading on HyperliquidX, with a 16-day campaign running from April 3 to April 19, offering rewards for trading.

3️⃣ **Starknet's STRK20s Launch**  
Starknet is preparing to launch STRK20s, a new privacy capability for confidential balances and private transfers, expected at the end of April. They also released Starkzap v2, adding five new DeFi modules to their SDK, enhancing bridging, swaps, and confidential transfers.

4️⃣ **Motoswap on Bitcoin L1**  
Motoswap has launched a full DeFi stack on Bitcoin L1 through OP_NET smart contracts, enabling swaps, staking, and farming natively on Bitcoin. This marks significant growth with over $26M in TVL flowing through OP_NET smart contracts.

5️⃣ **MezoNetwork's Token Launch**  
$MEZO is officially live, allowing users to claim and lock MEZO tokens alongside BTC for rewards. Institutions can custody $MEZO and unlock BitcoinFi yield through Anchorage Digital Bank or their self-custody wallet, Porto.

### Protocol Updates and Changes

1️⃣ **Rootstock's Vetiver 9.0.0 Upgrade**  
The Rootstock community has approved a set of consensus changes for the Vetiver 9.0.0 network upgrade, with mainnet activation at block 8,804,200 (~May 4th) and testnet activation around April 28th.

2️⃣ **Pell Network's 401(k) Inclusion**  
The US Government now allows Bitcoin in 401(k) retirement accounts. Pell Network facilitates restaking of BTC across chains for yield, enhancing Bitcoin's utility in traditional finance.

3️⃣ **Lightning Network's Taproot Update**  
Taproot Assets v0.7.2 has been released, improving quote handling, invoice settlement, and price oracle integration, further enhancing the Lightning Network's capabilities.

### Strategic Partnerships

1️⃣ **Solv Protocol's Governance Expansion**  
UOB Venture Management and The Spartan Group have joined Solv Protocol as Multi-Sig Governors, enhancing security and governance. This move underscores the importance of key asset holders in protocol governance.

### Metrics and Insights

1️⃣ **MAP Protocol's Cross-Chain Volume**  
MAP Protocol reports a cross-chain volume of $18,030,874.86 with 8,296 transactions, highlighting their growing ecosystem and successful DOGE & XRP cross-chain testing.

2️⃣ **TheTNetwork's tBTC DEX Activity**  
Q4 2025 saw BTC-denominated DEX activity rise about 4x QoQ, led by Curve on Ethereum, setting a benchmark for tBTC activity.
