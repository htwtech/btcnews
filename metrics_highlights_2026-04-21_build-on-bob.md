## Metrics and Performance Highlights

- **Transaction Volume on BOB Network**: The BOB network has reported a total of **46 million completed transactions**. This figure indicates substantial growth in user activity. For more details, refer to the [original tweet](https://x.com/build_on_bob/status/2045109036555944380) by @build_on_bob.

- **Economic Efficiency of BTC Transfers**: Transferring 1 BTC, valued at **$74,566**, incurs a fee of **$0.41** when converting to wBTC on @BNBCHAIN. This fee represents **0.00055%** of the transaction value, highlighting the economic efficiency of the network. Additional information can be found in the [tweet](https://x.com/build_on_bob/status/2044732045130444947) by @build_on_bob.
