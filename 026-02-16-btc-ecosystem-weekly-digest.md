# BTC Ecosystem Weekly Digest 

*February 9, 2026*

This week feels like a split reality.
While retail is busy farming badges and chatting with AI agents, the infrastructure layer is pivoting to real business models and institutional rails 🤔
Here is what’s actually happening:

## 1. [Citrea](https://citrea.xyz) and Farming Badges
They opened a vault with Keyrock offering ~4% APY.
The real motivation here is the "Yield Strategist" status and the "Early Adopter" badge. You aren't depositing for the yield, you are securing a multiplier for the future airdrop.

## 2. [BSquaredNetwork](https://www.bsquared.network) - AI Agents & Rewards
They launched "B² Mind," an on-chain autopilot, and they need your help to train it.
You can earn from the USDT pool by either chatting with the bot (easy mode) or building complex workflows (dev mode). It’s a good way to touch the AI narrative on Bitcoin without spending money.

## 3. [Babylon](https://babylonlabs.io) with Staking on Binance
$BABY staking is finally live on Simple Earn. It’s perfect for those who prefer keeping assets on CEXs, and a massive step for retail adoption.

## 4. [SolvProtocol](https://solv.finance) and Institutional Bridge
Solv just became a validator on Canton Network. It might sound boring, but this connects Bitcoin to over $6T in institutional capital.
While everyone is chasing meme coins, Solv is building the compliance rails to bring the "suits" and their liquidity into the ecosystem.

## 5. [Coredao_Org](https://coredao.org) with Pivot to Real Yield
The team officially announced they are done chasing vanity TVL metrics. The new focus is on a fee-based economy and real business.
They are already targeting the London Stock Exchange and building SatPay (a Bitcoin Neobank). It’s a healthy shift from grant farming to actual revenue.

## 6. [Rootstock](https://rootstock.io) expanding to Japan
The OG sidechain is quietly capturing the enterprise niche. They partnered with Animoca Brands Japan to onboard local corporations into BTC DeFi and tokenized $20M in RWA assets.
Slow and steady adoption by conservative capital.

## 7. [Midl](https://midl.xyz): On-Chain Luck
If you are tired of complex strategies, Midl is running a "Red Envelope" event for the Lunar New Year.
They used encrypted hashes to ensure the randomness is fair. It’s just a fun way to gamble for some Ordinals if you’re feeling lucky.

---

Retail is getting gamified engagement (Citrea, B²), while the protocols are building serious gateways for real money (Solv, Core, Rootstock).

Both are valid plays, just know which game you are playing.

See you next week! 🫡
