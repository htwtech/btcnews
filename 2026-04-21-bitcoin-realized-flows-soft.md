# Bitcoin is Recovering, But Realized Flows Are Still Soft

**Bitcoin is recovering, but realized flows are still soft.**

As of April 21, 2026, Net Realized Profit/Loss is hovering slightly below zero, around -$11.3M.

The point is not that the market is in heavy capitulation. It is not. The point is that realized profit has not taken control yet. After the large loss realization in February, flows have improved, but they are still struggling to hold a clean positive regime.

That suggests the recovery is still more about stress fading than fresh conviction returning. Sellers are no longer pressing as hard, but profit realization has not expanded enough to confirm stronger demand underneath.

**For us, that is the real takeaway. The bounce has helped repair the market, but the realized flow structure is still closer to neutral than strength.**
