# Killing the Bridge: How BOB Gateway Routes Native BTC

We’ve been tracking the BOB ecosystem for a minute. The team keeps shipping actual infrastructure, and their BOB Gateway warrants a hard look—specifically regarding how it routes capital and where the real yield sits.

Shoving native BTC into DeFi has always been a fragmented nightmare. You usually end up juggling a dozen wrappers, eating bridge risk, and scavenging for gas tokens across destination chains. Gateway tries to kill that friction entirely using an intent-based architecture.

## Abstracting the EVM Drag

From the user's side, the bridge is effectively dead. You just sign a single transaction on the Bitcoin network declaring a specific intent—like dumping BTC for stables on Base or aping directly into a yield vault. 

All the technical heavy lifting gets outsourced to off-chain solvers. And because the whole engine relies on cryptographic proofs, the flow stays strictly non-custodial.

> Massive amounts of Bitcoin liquidity remain stagnant simply because the barrier to entry for DeFi is too high. Gateway essentially narrows the gap, offering a CEX-like user experience while maintaining self-custody.

## The Solver Arbitrage Play

The actual money to be made here sits with the liquidity providers. 

BOB is gunning for sub-10-second execution. That means someone has to front the EVM assets while your native Bitcoin transaction is still rotting in the mempool. Solvers step in and capture the fees for underwriting that time delay, effectively setting up a highly scalable, market-neutral yield play.

Meanwhile, regular users just save on friction. Bypassing the usual maze of bridge tolls and gas conversions directly pads your net farming APY. 

## Bolting Native Deposits to Any Chain

Zooming out, Gateway’s SDK lets dApps across 11+ chains plug native BTC deposits straight into their front ends. If this scales out, BOB pivots into a primary liquidity rail, dragging the usual TVL tailwinds—early pool incentives and ecosystem rewards—along with it.

Intents are undeniably the current meta. If their solver network can absorb heavy market volatility without snapping under the pressure, Gateway is positioned to seriously reshape the BTCFi plumbing.
