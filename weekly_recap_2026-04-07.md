## Infrastructure and Protocol Updates

1️⃣ **BOB Unveils Instant BTC Swaps**

@build_on_bob launched a new product offering instant and fixed-rate BTC swaps. With execution under 10 seconds and zero slippage, this feature enhances trading efficiency in the Bitcoin ecosystem. Additionally, BOB integrated 133 PRs in March, adding 27 new features and fixing 36 bugs, demonstrating robust development momentum.

2️⃣ **Rootstock's Vetiver 9.0.0 Upgrade**

Rootstock has approved consensus changes for Vetiver 9.0.0, with mainnet activation slated for block 8,804,200 (~May 4th). This upgrade aims to enhance the network's protocol efficiency and security.

3️⃣ **Starknet's DeFi Expansion**

Starknet introduced Starkzap v2, adding five new DeFi modules to its SDK. This includes bridging, swaps, DCA, lending, and confidential transfers, significantly broadening the DeFi capabilities on the network. The introduction of a post-quantum proving system and recursive circuit proving marks a leap towards quantum resistance and efficiency.

4️⃣ **OP_NET Smart Contracts on Bitcoin L1**

@opnetbtc has successfully launched OP_NET smart contracts on Bitcoin L1, facilitating native DeFi activities. With $26M+ already flowing through these contracts, Motoswap has deployed a full DeFi stack, enabling swaps, staking, and farming directly on Bitcoin's base layer.

5️⃣ **Babylon's Trustless Bitcoin Vaults**

Babylon has launched Trustless Bitcoin Vaults, enabling native Bitcoin borrowing on Aave v4. This innovation brings Bitcoin collateral to Ethereum, enhancing interoperability and utility across chains.

## Ecosystem Growth and Strategic Moves


7️⃣ **Coredao's Yield-Bearing Bitcoin on LSE**

Yield-bearing Bitcoin is now trading on the London Stock Exchange, powered by Core. This product enables traditional investors to access Bitcoin yields without needing a crypto wallet, marking a significant step towards mainstream adoption.

8️⃣  **Solv Protocol's Institutional Partnerships**

Solv Protocol has partnered with prominent firms like Gumi Cryptos and The Spartan Group to safeguard Bitcoin Finance, focusing on Institutional Decentralized Finance. This collaboration underscores the growing institutional interest in Bitcoin-based financial products.

9️⃣**Utexo's Privacy-First Payments**

Utexo has launched a product offering private payments with privacy-first KYC, using portable ZK-based ID verification on Lightning. This innovation aims to enhance user privacy while complying with regulatory standards.
