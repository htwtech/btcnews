## Your Bitcoin Is Losing Money Just Sitting There. The Solution from Syntetika

![Alt text here](https://bvkmqlnhnryztqknjjah.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/content/news/syntetika_main.webp)

Syntetika is a protocol that turns Bitcoin into a yield-bearing asset by packaging institutional strategies from the Nasdaq-listed Hilbert Group into transparent DeFi vaults.

Previously, you were stuck with a choice: either keep your BTC in cold storage and earn nothing, or risk it all on questionable bridges for a fraction of a percent.

But now, as the BTCFi narrative takes center stage, the rules are changing. A new player has entered the scene, combining DeFi liquidity with institutional discipline.

## When Nasdaq Comes to DeFi

The project’s key ace is its partnership with Hilbert Group. This isn’t a team of anonymous developers with anime PFPs; it is a regulated quantitative asset manager traded on Nasdaq (HILB).

For us, this means one thing: a level of security that is typically unattainable for standard DeFi projects. A public company is required to undergo regular audits and bears legal responsibility.

This creates a layer of accountability that significantly reduces the classic “rug pull” risk in which anonymous teams disappear with the treasury.

The Hilbert team has honed their delta-neutral strategies on billion-dollar volumes for years, and now these institutional-grade tools are available not just to funds, but to retail users.

## Where Does the Yield Come From?

Forget complex schemes. Everything here is built on the ERC-4626 standard.

You don’t send your BTC to a developer’s hot wallet; you send it to top-tier custodians like Copper or Xapo Bank. In return, you receive hBTC — tokenized Bitcoin backed 1:1.

Next, you receive shBTC. This is an asset that appreciates in value relative to the underlying Bitcoin. The Basis+ strategy generates the yield. This is classic delta-neutral arbitrage: the algorithms profit from funding rates and the spread between spot and futures prices.

As long as the market is active and volatile, the strategy extracts profit. It’s math, not artificial inflation.

## Beyond and $105M in Liquidity

For this machine to run, it needs fuel and roads.

The fuel issue is solved: Caddy Finance has already provided a liquidity commitment of $105M. This means shBTC isn’t just numbers on a screen, but a liquid asset ready for immediate use in DeFi.

The “roads” situation became even more interesting following the recent partnership announcement with Beyond. Think of it as “LayerZero for Bitcoin.”

This integration allows Syntetika’s liquidity to flow through Beyond’s infrastructure, maximizing yield and simplifying access for institutions. This transforms the project from a local story into a cross-chain player.

## $SYNT Tokenomics

The team understands that a governance token shouldn’t just be a tool for voting on “logo colors.” That’s why Syntetika utilizes the time-tested ve-model (similar to Curve or Aerodrome).

The mechanism is simple: you lock $SYNT for up to a year and receive a veSYNT NFT. This gives you not only voting power but also a share of the platform’s real revenue.

The protocol uses a portion of its profits to buy back the token from the market and distribute it to stakers. This creates a closed value loop where the token is backed by cash flow, not promises.
