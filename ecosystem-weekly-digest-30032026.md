# BTC Ecosystem Weekly Digest

Capital is quietly rotating into compliance and actual yield. We are seeing a hard pivot from speculative airdrop farming to compliant infrastructure and institutional rails. Here is the alpha for the week.

## Citrea launches Merkl incentive campaign

It has been exactly a month since [Citrea](https://x.com/citrea_xyz) hit mainnet, locking in the core liquidity routes. To scale up, they just launched a 30-day Merkl incentive campaign for the cBTC-ctUSD pool on Satsuma DEX, allowing easy single-sided deposits via Ichi vaults.

## Lombard Season 2 claims go live

[Lombard Finance](https://x.com/Lombard_Finance) Season 2 $BARD claims are live today, and the 55% APY staking bonus window closes tonight. Note the Season 3 meta shift: passive holding is dead, you only earn points by actively deploying LBTC or BTC.b.

## SEC clears SolvBTC regulatory status

The SEC just gave the ecosystem a massive regulatory gift by clarifying that wrapped tokens like SolvBTC are not securities. [Solv Protocol](https://x.com/SolvProtocol) is immediately leveraging this to push compliant institutional BTCFi.

## BOB targets centralized swap volume

[BOB](https://x.com/build_on_bob) is going after the $800B centralized swap volume with Stage 1 of the Bank of Bitcoin. They are executing instant, fixed-rate native BTC swaps with zero slippage using zero-conf technology.

## Babylon integrates Trustless Vaults with Ledger

[Babylon](https://x.com/babylonlabs_io) is rolling out Ledger support for Trustless Bitcoin Vaults. Instead of messing with hot wallets, you can now authorize vault operations and deploy capital directly from a hardware screen you already trust for self-custody.

## Beyond caps Echoports supply at 940

[Beyond](https://x.com/beyond__tech) officially closed the Echoports mint, locking the final supply at a tight 940. With the vast majority of these Ordinals already locked up on Best in Slot to farm the $BYD drop, expect secondary trading to open soon with a very thin float.

## Circle deploys USDCx on Movement

Bringing trust-minimized cross-chain liquidity without relying on sketchy third-party bridges. The actual alpha here is the Day 1 integration rollout - major ecosystem apps like [Avalon Finance](https://x.com/avalonfinance_), LayerBank, and Echelon are supporting it immediately, meaning you can put that liquidity to work right away.

## Mezo unveils 2026 institutional roadmap

[Mezo Network](https://x.com/MezoNetwork) dropped their 2026 roadmap. The entire focus is scaling their $MUSD stablecoin cross-chain and setting up institutional-grade minting and custody through Anchorage Digital.

## Fiamma upgrades Ride with instant markets

[Fiamma](https://x.com/fiamma_labs) just pushed a major update for their Ride app. They added one-tap execution for 5 and 15-minute crypto markets, alongside a daily check-in and lottery system to farm XP. Update the app to start stacking.

## BounceBit opens stablecoin yield pool

[BounceBit](https://x.com/bouncebit) opened a straightforward stablecoin yield play on BNB Chain. It is a strictly capped $1M USDT pool paying a fixed 7% APR for a 60-day lock.

The meta is maturing fast. Protocols are openly cutting off passive holders and only rewarding active capital deployment across DeFi. We are tracking this structural shift and adjusting our strategy to follow where the real volume is heading.
