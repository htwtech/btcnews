Stacks is showcasing significant growth and activity within its ecosystem. Notably, the total value locked (TVL) in sBTC has reached $305 million, with over 750 AI agents actively transacting on the platform. This indicates a robust and expanding ecosystem.

The platform has also seen $1 billion in transactions within just six weeks, highlighting strong user demand, particularly for the @bitflow service. This milestone underscores the increasing interest in leveraging Bitcoin for more diverse financial activities.

USDCx's integration on Allbridge Core now facilitates a direct path to Bitcoin DeFi on Stacks, enhancing liquidity and user experience across 24 supported chains. This development promises lower fees and a more seamless user experience.

In addition, $1 billion has been processed through HODLMM, and Zest has surpassed 880 sBTC in TVL, marking a significant achievement for the platform.

Finally, the launch of hBTC, a Bitcoin native earn vault on Stacks, offers multiple yield sources, including from @Strategy's USDSTRC, providing users with native BTC transactions.
