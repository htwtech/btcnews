# TradFi Eats the Float

This is the next issue in our series on where capital is moving across Bitcoin, L2s, BTCFi infrastructure, and BTC-linked capital vehicles.

Private rounds were limited this time, but the broader flow was still telling. Capital kept moving through accelerators, treasury companies, ETFs, and investment products.

**The deals / flows, Apr 16–23, 2026:**

BitcoinFi Accelerator x Fairmint — $26M in startup funding equity brought natively onchain across the latest BitcoinFi cohort (Draper Associates, Draper Dragon, Thesis, Supernormal Foundation)

@Strategy — 34,164 BTC acquired for ~$2.54B (avg. ~$74,395 per BTC)

@_ALCPB — 12 BTC acquired for €0.8M, bringing total holdings to 2,937 BTC

Morgan Stanley MSBT — over $139M in BTC accumulated within nine days of launch

Bitcoin investment products — $1.116B weekly inflows

U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs — $996.4M weekly net inflows, the strongest week since mid-January (IBIT led with ~$906M)

The flow was not centered on new private rounds this time. It came through the wrappers around BTC: accelerator-backed equity, corporate treasuries, bank-issued ETFs, and listed investment products.

**Public markets are doing more of the work now, making it easier for institutions to hold Bitcoin exposure at scale.**

*The next question is whether Bitcoin-native rails can turn that exposure into actual use - lending, settlement, collateral, payments, and yield.*
