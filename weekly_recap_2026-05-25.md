## Product Launches and Integrations

1️⃣ **Starknet's strkBTC Launch**  
@Starknet has introduced strkBTC, a ZK-powered shielded Bitcoin, on their Layer 2 network. This marks a significant advancement for Bitcoin's privacy and utility in the DeFi space.

2️⃣ **Zest Protocol's TGE**  
$ZEST is now live on Tenero and trading globally on exchanges such as @binance and @kucoin. Zest Protocol aims to address BTC liquidity challenges by enabling self-custodial borrowing against Bitcoin.

3️⃣ **BOB Gateway Swap Functionality**  
@build_on_bob Gateway now allows users to swap native BTC directly from their CEX accounts without needing a Bitcoin wallet.

4️⃣ **RiverdotInc's satUSD Payment Integration**  
@satUSD is now integrated with @yodlpay, enabling stablecoin transactions in Vietnam, Brazil, Argentina, and the Philippines.

5️⃣ **Hemi's hBitVM Launch**  
@hemi_xyz has launched hBitVM, offering ZK-secured settlement for Bitcoin with application-specific use cases.

6️⃣ **BitSafe Finance Trading Launch**  
Bitcoin trading is now available on @Kora_Protocol using CBTC, expanding BTC trading opportunities.

7️⃣ **Spice Flow Integration with ZentraFinance**  
@spicenetio's Spice Flow is now integrated with @ZentraFinance, facilitating asset usage from any EVM chain.

8️⃣ **0DFinance's 0dAMM Launch**  
@0DFinance has launched 0dAMM exclusively on @avnu_fi, introducing the first PropAMM on Starknet.

## Protocol Changes and Upgrades

1️⃣ **Lombard Finance's Migration to Chainlink CCIP**  
@LombardFinance has migrated to Chainlink CCIP to enhance the security of over $1B in Bitcoin assets.

2️⃣ **Stacks' Bitcoin Staking Proposal**  
@Stacks has introduced a new Bitcoin Staking model aimed at providing a more grounded approach to earning yield on Bitcoin.

3️⃣ **SolvProtocol's Cross-Chain Operations Update**  
@SolvProtocol is closing burn-mint permissions for selected assets, urging users to withdraw underlying assets by the end of June.

4️⃣ **TheTNetwork's tBTC Minting Update**  
@TheTNetwork has temporarily paused Optimistic Minting due to security concerns, with mints now routing through a sweeping mechanism.

## Partnerships and Collaborations

1️⃣ **Hemi and AccountableData Partnership**  
@hemi_xyz has partnered with AccountableData to implement continuous Proof of Solvency for hemiBTC.

2️⃣ **Arkade and HodlHodl Collaboration**

3️⃣ **utexocom and SolvProtocol Partnership**

4️⃣ **CredShields as Canton Network's Audit Partner**
