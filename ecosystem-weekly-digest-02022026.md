# BTC Ecosystem Weekly Digest 

Judgement day for Mezo, "points" hints from Citrea, and a protocol that pays you to borrow money. The infra is getting complex, and the rewards are getting real. Let's dive in:

## [Citrea](https://citrea.xyz) Reveals Mainnet Structure
They are using "Clementine" (BitVM + ZK) with a 1-of-N honest security model. Much safer than your standard multisig! More importantly: the dashboard is live. It tracks contributions and badges, which usually translates to "future rewards." Start building your on-chain history.

## [MezoNetwork](https://bankfree.mezo.org) Airdrop Checker is LIVE
The moment of truth. Go to the checker and see what you cooked. Liquidity is thin, so manage your expectations, but at least now you know if the deposit was worth the lock-up.

## [ZestProtocol](https://www.zestprotocol.com) V2: Get Paid to Borrow?
They just launched V2 with 1.5M USDCx. Collateral yield means you essentially get paid to borrow stablecoins. Negative interest rates on Bitcoin DeFi. If you want to understand the mechanics, catch their Town Hall on Feb 4.

## [b14g_network](https://b14g.network) x Babylon Flywheel
b14g is burning $BABY, 50k queued, targeting 150k burn by Q1 2026. Stake BABY (~40% APY) → Protocol Revenue → Buyback & Burn. Finally, some sustainable value accrual instead of just emission dumping.

## [BSquaredNetwork](https://www.bsquared.network) Trading Comp ($200k Pool)
Top 2000 buyers get 65 $B2—at current prices, that's ~$55. Unless you are already holding or trading huge volume, don't burn gas chasing this. It’s a nice bonus for whales, not a life-changer for retail. Deadline is Feb 5.

## [Coredao_Org](https://coredao.org) SatPay Waitlist
Over 20,000 people signed up for the Bitcoin neobank. The "Living on a Bitcoin Standard" narrative is gaining traction.

## [Spicenet](https://spicenet.io) Goes Live
Official Telegram is up. If you are tracking the project, get in early to catch announcements.
