## Infrastructure and Protocol Updates

1️⃣ **BOB Gateway Expands Cross-Chain Swaps**

BOB Gateway has launched native BTC to BTCB, LBTC, and tBTC swaps across multiple chains, enhancing cross-chain liquidity. Additionally, they have integrated with @leodexio to power native BTC swaps. This move signifies a step forward in interoperability within the Bitcoin ecosystem.

2️⃣ **Lombard Finance Shifts LBTC Backing**

LBTC's backing is transitioning into the Bitwise covered-call strategy, starting with a pilot of 150 BTC. This strategic move aims to optimize asset utilization and provide enhanced yield opportunities.

3️⃣ **Avalon Labs Launches New Yield Vaults**

Avalon Labs has introduced a Market-Neutral Yield Vault and a Delta Neutral Yield Vault, targeting approximately 15% APY. The Market-Neutral Vault has already attracted $35.08M in deposits within five days. Furthermore, $AVL is now listed on @krakenlistings, broadening access to their offerings.

4️⃣ **Stacks Announces Bitcoin Staking Launch**

Bitcoin Staking on Stacks is set to go live in about 19 days, facilitated by STX. The partnership with HashKey Cloud for the first Genesis Bond and the expansion of the sBTC signer set with Ankr and The Tie highlight Stacks' commitment to enhancing its staking infrastructure.

5️⃣ **Starknet Achieves Quantum-Safe Bitcoin Transaction**

Starknet has mined the first quantum-safe Bitcoin transaction, marking a significant milestone in blockchain security. Additionally, nearly 150 projects are leveraging STRK20, Starknet's privacy framework, for shielded balances and private transfers.

## Ecosystem Growth and Partnerships

6️⃣ **Avalon Labs Expands Bitcoin Lending**

Avalon Labs is extending its Bitcoin lending capabilities to @pharos_network with @FunctionBTC, bringing institutional-grade lending infrastructure to RealFi. This expansion signifies growing institutional interest in Bitcoin-backed financial products.

7️⃣ **Rootstock's Strategic Moves**

@RootstockLabs participated alongside major industry players in Hong Kong, showcasing its advancements. The platform is moving beyond bridge aggregation with plans for institutional custody integrations and advanced transaction workflows.

8️⃣ **Radiant Nuclear Secures U.S. Army Contract**

Radiant Nuclear has secured a $750 million contract from the U.S. Army to deliver 15 Kaleidos microreactors. This partnership underscores the growing intersection between blockchain technology and traditional industries.

## Product Launches and Integrations

9️⃣ **RiverdotInc Launches satUSD on Tangem**

RiverdotInc has launched satUSD on @Tangem, enabling secure storage of satUSD in just 30 seconds. This product launch enhances the usability of stablecoins within the Bitcoin ecosystem.

🔟 **BitSafe_Finance Expands CBTC Offerings**

CBTC is now live on @SwapMonster_ across four markets. The integration with @CanPayAI allows AI agents to hold Bitcoin-backed value, highlighting the innovative use of AI in financial transactions.
